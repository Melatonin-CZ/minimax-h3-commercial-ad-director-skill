# Worked Examples — Four Certified Commercial Ad Prompts

Each example pairs one structure with one category playbook from `commercial_ad_styles.md`, follows `timeline_methodology.md`, and is compressed into the exact T2VA syntax from `VIDEO_PROMPT_WRITING_GUIDE_base_en.md`. Durations vary (15s, 12s, 15s, 6s) to show the system isn't tied to any one length. All four charts sum exactly to their target duration.

---

## Example A — Hook-Problem-Solution-CTA × FMCG/Household (15s)

**Choices:** structure = Hook-Problem-Solution-CTA, category = FMCG/Household (stain-remover spray). End-card reserved first (3.0s), hook budgeted second (2.0s), body fills the remaining 10.0s as three claim-cut beats.

**0.1s timing chart**

| t (s) | shot | camera | subject / text | note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–2.0 | 1 | handheld close | red wine spills across a white shirt in slow motion; a mother's hand freezes mid-reach | hook, visual problem, no VO yet |
| 2.0 | 2 | cut | — | new claim beat begins |
| 2.0–5.0 | 2 | static, small Push In | text "REMOVES STAINS OTHERS CAN'T" appears; spray applied directly to the stain, worked in with a brush | claim 1, 3.0s |
| 5.0 | 3 | cut | — | new claim beat |
| 5.0–8.5 | 3 | static medium | text "WORKS IN COLD WATER TOO" appears; the shirt goes into a washing machine on a cold-wash cycle | claim 2, 3.5s |
| 8.5 | 4 | cut | — | new claim beat, proof |
| 8.5–12.0 | 4 | static, small Push In | text "GONE IN ONE WASH" appears; the mother lifts the clean shirt out, stain fully gone, holds it up to the light | claim 3 / proof, 3.5s |
| 12.0 | 5 | cut | — | reserved end-card beat begins |
| 12.0–15.0 | 5 | static | product bottle centered, logo, text "SHOP NOW — 20% OFF TODAY" holds | end-card, 3.0s, ≥1.0s fully static and legible |

**Certified prompt**
```
integrated_multimodal_description: [Shot 1] Live-action, bright high-key FMCG lighting, a handheld close shot captures red wine spilling across a white shirt in slow motion as a mother's hand freezes mid-reach toward it. [Shot 2] At 00:02.000, the shot cuts to a static medium shot with a small push in as text reading "REMOVES STAINS OTHERS CAN'T" appears in bold sans-serif; she sprays the stain remover directly onto the stain and works it in with a brush. [Shot 3] At 00:05.000, the shot cuts to a static medium shot as text reading "WORKS IN COLD WATER TOO" appears; she loads the shirt into a washing machine and starts a cold-wash cycle. [Shot 4] At 00:08.500, the shot cuts to a static shot with a small push in as text reading "GONE IN ONE WASH" appears; she lifts the clean shirt from the machine, the stain completely gone, and holds it up to the light. [Shot 5] At 00:12.000, the shot cuts to a static shot of the product bottle centered against a bright colored background beside the brand logo, as text reading "SHOP NOW — 20% OFF TODAY" appears and holds.

overall_soundscape: Wine splashes wetly against fabric, the spray bottle triggers with a sharp hiss, the washing machine hums and fills, and a soft satisfied exhale accompanies the final reveal.

non_diegetic_music: An upbeat, high-energy pop instrumental at a fast tempo, with a bright percussive stinger landing on each claim's on-screen text appearance and a final triumphant swell under the end-card.
```

**Certification note:** the end-card's 3.0s was reserved before the body was planned, per the ad-specific sequencing rule — the three claims fill exactly what's left rather than being planned first and squeezing the CTA. Every claim's on-screen text is exact and paired with an explicit cutaway per the claim-to-cutaway causality rule.

## Example B — Product Hero Showcase × Tech/Gadget (12s)

**Choices:** structure = Product Hero Showcase, category = Tech/Gadget (wireless earbuds). Soft close, no hard CTA — sequenced normally (no reserved end-card time).

**0.1s timing chart**

| t (s) | shot | camera | subject | note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.5 | 1 | static | earbud charging case, closed, floating in neutral-cool negative space | cold open, 1.5s |
| 1.5 | 2 | cut | — | reveal begins |
| 1.5–4.0 | 2 | Arc Shot, small amplitude, slow speed | camera arcs a partial turn around the case, rim-light tracing its edges | reveal, 2.5s |
| 4.0 | 3 | cut | — | detail beat |
| 4.0–6.5 | 3 | static, Push In, small amplitude, slow speed | macro push toward the case's hinge, focus pulling across the brushed-metal texture | macro detail, 2.5s |
| 6.5 | 4 | cut | — | demo beat |
| 6.5–9.0 | 4 | static | the lid opens, an earbud lifts free, a charging-status light blinks once | feature demo, 2.5s |
| 9.0 | 5 | cut | — | hero hold begins |
| 9.0–12.0 | 5 | static | both earbuds stand upright beside the open case; the wordmark fades in beside them | hero hold + wordmark, 3.0s, soft close |

**Certified prompt**
```
integrated_multimodal_description: [Shot 1] Live-action, neutral-cool studio lighting with multi-point rim backlighting, a static shot frames a closed wireless-earbud charging case floating in negative space against a seamless gradient background. [Shot 2] At 00:01.500, the shot cuts as the camera arcs around the case with small amplitude at slow speed, rim light tracing its edges as it turns. [Shot 3] At 00:04.000, the shot cuts to a static shot that pushes in with small amplitude at slow speed toward the case's hinge, the focus pulling across the brushed-metal texture. [Shot 4] At 00:06.500, the shot cuts to a static shot as the lid opens, an earbud lifts free on its own, and the charging-status light blinks once. [Shot 5] At 00:09.000, the shot cuts to a static shot of both earbuds standing upright beside the open case as the brand wordmark fades in beside them and the frame holds.

overall_soundscape: A soft magnetic click as the lid opens, a subtle electronic chime synced to the charging light's blink, and a low, clean studio room tone throughout.

non_diegetic_music: A minimal, restrained ambient synth pad at a slow tempo, understated and unhurried, swelling only slightly under the final wordmark hold.
```

**Certification note:** five shots across 12s (every ~2.4s) matches the showcase's "measured, deliberate" convention — faster than a lifestyle piece, slower than a DR ad's claim-cutting. No CTA text or urgency language anywhere, per the structure's soft-close rule.

## Example C — Testimonial/UGC-Style × Beauty/Skincare (15s)

**Choices:** structure = Testimonial/UGC-style, category = Beauty/Skincare. Single continuous handheld shot per the format's authenticity convention — narrative stages charted as continuous spans, not chopped into many rows.

**0.1s timing chart**

| t (s) | shot | camera | subject / text | note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.5 | 1 | handheld, slight natural drift | young woman speaks directly to camera in natural window light, no makeup | hook line, 1.5s |
| 1.5–4.5 | 1 | handheld, slight natural drift | continues speaking, describing her skin frustration | backstory, 3.0s |
| 4.5–8.0 | 1 | handheld, slight natural drift | holds up the product, applies a small amount to her cheek | discovery/solution, 3.5s |
| 8.0–11.5 | 1 | handheld, slight natural drift | turns cheek toward camera, natural light catching visibly smoother skin | proof, 3.5s |
| 11.5–15.0 | 1 | handheld, slight natural drift | delivers the CTA line directly to camera; caption text appears matching her words | CTA, 3.5s |

**Certified prompt**
```
integrated_multimodal_description: [Shot 1] Live-action, natural available window light, true-to-color, a handheld selfie-style shot with slight natural drift frames a young woman speaking directly to camera, bare-faced, in a bright bedroom. The relaxed, conversational young woman (S1) says: <d>[English] Okay, I need to talk about what happened to my skin.</d> She continues, describing her frustration with breakouts, <d>[English] I tried literally everything and nothing worked until this.</d> She holds up a small skincare jar and applies a small amount to her cheek with two fingers, <d>[English] It's this calming balm, and I use it every night now.</d> She turns her cheek toward the camera, natural light catching visibly smoother, calmer skin. <d>[English] Look at that, no redness, no texture.</d> She looks back to the lens for the close, <d>[English] Link's in my bio, you have to try it.</d> Native-style captions matching her spoken words appear at the bottom of the frame throughout.

overall_soundscape: Quiet bedroom room tone with the faint rustle of fabric as she moves, and a soft tap as the jar lid is set down.

non_diegetic_music: N/A
```

**Certification note:** zero cuts for the full 15s, matching the format's single-fixed-creator-framing convention; the natural handheld drift is deliberate, not a flaw, per the authenticity-over-polish rule. Captions run continuously rather than as a separate end-card beat, since UGC-style ads conventionally fold the CTA into the same continuous take.

## Example D — 6-Second Bumper × Food & Beverage (6s)

**Choices:** structure = 6-Second Bumper, category = Food & Beverage (sparkling iced tea). Single unbroken shot, CTA embedded in the one message, no separate end-card beat.

**0.1s timing chart**

| t (s) | shot | camera | subject / text | note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.0 | 1 | static | bottle with visible label enters frame as a glass waits below; product visible from the first frame | brand/product visible within 1.0s |
| 1.0–4.0 | 1 | static, small Push In | backlit pour begins, condensation beading on the glass, fizz catching the light in slow motion | sensory hero moment, 3.0s |
| 4.0–6.0 | 1 | static | glass settles, full and fizzing; text "GRAB ONE TODAY" appears and holds | embedded CTA, 2.0s |

**Certified prompt**
```
integrated_multimodal_description: [Shot 1] Live-action, backlit warm saturated lighting, a static shot with a small push in frames a sparkling iced tea bottle with its label clearly visible beside an empty glass, the bottle already in frame at 0.00 seconds. The pour begins, backlit so the amber liquid glows and the rising fizz catches the light in slow motion, condensation beading visibly on the glass. The glass settles, full and still fizzing gently, as text reading "GRAB ONE TODAY" appears in bold, mobile-legible type and holds to the end of the frame.

overall_soundscape: The pour fizzes and glugs steadily, with a light carbonation crackle as it settles in the glass.

non_diegetic_music: A short, bright single musical sting, rising then resolving cleanly within the six seconds.
```

**Certification note:** one shot, no internal cuts, product visible from frame one — all per the bumper's hard constraints. The CTA text is embedded directly in the closing 2.0s of the single message rather than built as a separate end-card shot, since a 6-second bumper doesn't have room for a distinct closing beat.

## What these four demonstrate together

- The end-card reservation rule (Example A) versus the soft-close structures (Example B) versus the embedded-CTA bumper (Example D) show three genuinely different closing strategies, chosen by structure rather than applied uniformly.
- Example C shows that "0.1s precision" doesn't mean "many short rows" — a testimonial's authenticity depends on long continuous spans, and the chart reflects that instead of artificially fragmenting it.
- Every category playbook's lighting/grade language is present without altering its paired structure's cut rhythm or pacing, per the combining rule in `commercial_ad_styles.md`.
